# F015 – Context-aware tone

**Type**: Feature
**Epic**: E001

## Summary

Buddy adjusts the user's chosen tone to their time of day (morning, midday, evening). Replies stay short in day-to-day chat, and longer coaching is kept for planning and reflection.

## Problem / Motivation

The right voice at 8 AM is wrong at 10 PM. Long coaching replies get in the way of quick day-to-day check-ins.

## Users

Signed-in users.

## What the user should be able to do

- Get crisp, decisive replies in the morning, objective re-planning at midday and calm wind-down replies in the evening.
- Get short answers in day-to-day chat and deeper coaching when planning or reflecting.

## Rules & Constraints

- Uses the user's local time.
- The chosen tone stays recognisable.

## Out of Scope

- Off-track / streak-break detection. It waits for analytics metrics.
