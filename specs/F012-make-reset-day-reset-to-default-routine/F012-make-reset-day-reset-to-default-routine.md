## Summary

<!-- One or two sentences: what do you want to build? -->
Reset day replaces the selected day's tasks with a fresh copy of its default routine (weekend day → Weekend routine), after the user confirms. On the Onboarding/Default routine pages, a button copies all Weekday tasks to the Weekend routine.
