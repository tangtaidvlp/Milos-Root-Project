# F014 – Buddy tone

**Type**: Feature
**Epic**: E001

## Summary

Users pick Buddy's tone on the Personal Target page: Balanced (default) or one of 5 archetypes. Buddy replies in that tone in every chat.

## Problem / Motivation

People need different coaching styles. One fixed voice feels either too soft or too pushy.

## Users

Signed-in users.

## What the user should be able to do

- See the 6 tones with their vibe, best use and an example, and pick one.
- Get Buddy replies in the chosen tone.

## Rules & Constraints

- Each tone is a fixed preset of 4 axes (directiveness, warmth, strictness, length). The user doesn't tune the axes.
- No tone uses blame or guilt language.
- Tone changes wording only, never what Buddy is allowed to do.

## Out of Scope

- Time-of-day tone shifts (F015). Off-track detection (later).
