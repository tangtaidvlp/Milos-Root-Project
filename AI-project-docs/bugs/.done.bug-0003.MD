# bug-0003: Refresh tokens expire after 15 minutes instead of 7 days

- **Found**: 2026-11-23, F008 (research R7)
- **Severity**: Medium (users are signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity; silent re-auth fails)
- **Where**: `service/AuthenticationService.login`

## What happens
`login` sets the refresh-token expiry with `accessTokenExpiration` (900 s). `refreshTokenExpiration` (604800 s, 7 days) is never used.

## Reproduction
Component test: log in, `clock.advance(Duration.ofMinutes(16))`, then `POST /api/v1/auth/refresh` with the cookie → 401 "Your session has expired". It should be 200 until 7 days have passed.

## Expected
Refresh token and cookie both last `jwt.refresh.expiration` (7 days).

F008 left a `NOTE` comment on the line and kept the behaviour (no change outside FR-018).
