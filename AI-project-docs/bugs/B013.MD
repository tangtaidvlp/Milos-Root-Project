# B013: The timeline board ignores its `label`

- **Found**: 2026-11-26, T001 review (step 1)
- **Severity**: Low (accessibility: screen readers always hear "Timeline board"; in Compare the two boards sit inside sections already labelled "Plan" and "Reality")
- **Where**: `ui-personal-routine-manager/src/features/board/components/EditingBoard.tsx` (`aria-label="Timeline board"`)

## What happens
`label` is declared in `BoardProps` but `EditingBoard` ignores it. Labels passed by pages are dropped: "Day plan" / "Reality" (`DailyRoutinePage.tsx`), "Plan" / "Reality" (`CompareBoards.jsx`).

## Reproduction
Open Today and inspect the board's `<section>`: `aria-label="Timeline board"` instead of "Day plan".

## Expected
The board uses `label` as its accessible name, with "Timeline board" as the default.

## Notes
Changing it changes what screen readers announce and may need e2e selector updates (`getByRole('region', { name: 'Timeline board' })`).
